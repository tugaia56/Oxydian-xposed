Added:
- New build for pure Android ROMs (crDroid, Android 17...): Oxydian-Android-release-X.apk, with only Settings and SystemUI in its LSPosed scope. The OxygenOS build (Oxydian-release-X.apk) keeps its full scope
- Why Android 17 needs the module: Oxydian Theme works with overlays, which can only swap resources. Things that Android draws in code cannot be reached by an overlay, so on Android 17 the module hooks them: the transparent Settings window, the status bar colours (SystemUI tints icons and clock in code and draws the battery percentage with Compose), the notification background tint and the shade opacity
- Android 17: Settings window shows the wallpaper behind the pages when a Transparency background is chosen in Oxydian Theme
- Android 17: notification panel / shade opacity chosen in the Theme. The module also stops the NaN crash of SystemUI (BackgroundBlurAlphaSync) that crashed SystemUI at every start and put LSPosed in safe mode
- Android 17: one colour for status bar icons and clock, plus separate clock colour and battery percentage colour, chosen in the Theme. The crDroid clock chip outline follows the Theme accent, also right after boot
- Android 17: notification styles from the Theme (system tint and crDroid blur removed when a style is chosen)
- OxygenOS: status bar clock, battery percentage and classic icons (Do Not Disturb, alarm, Bluetooth...) follow the Theme colours; battery and charging icon follow the icon colour
- OxygenOS: Wallpapers and style page: Always-on display / Font / Other cards use the system background (the grey was a black layer under a 15% white cover)
- Colour mods for more apps (add them to the LSPosed scope): Box, Call Recorder, Claude, Dreo, Drive, F-Droid, APKMirror, Discord, Alexa
- Notification manager: the "Schedules" edit bar follows the theme background; COUI switches stay visible when foreground and background are equal
Changed:
- Mods start once per package (Discord loaded another plugin first and blocked the app's own mods)
- On non-OxygenOS ROMs no global hooks are installed (zygote/resources)
- OxygenOS quick settings header clock: old clock containers are dropped (the list kept growing and slowed SystemUI)
Removed:
- Hooks now covered by the Theme: Droid-ify, APKUpdater and Drive surface colours
