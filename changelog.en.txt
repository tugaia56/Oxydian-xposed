Added:
- Google Messages: new look for chat bubbles, with its own page in Mods. Sent bubbles can take the accent colour at 50% transparency (or be dark like the received ones), every bubble can get a thin border with its own colour for received and sent (accent or any colour you pick), and the bottom corner can be sharp (right on sent bubbles, left on received). To use it, tick Messages in the LSPosed scope of Oxydian
